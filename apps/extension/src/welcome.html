Your camera bubble is ready

When you record your screen with SureFrame, your camera floats in a bubble over your web pages, with Pause and Stop beside it. Drag it anywhere, and drag its corner to resize it.

Allow your camera once here, so the bubble never has to ask on each website.

The extension doesn't record, save or send anything. Your recording stays in the SureFrame tab, encrypted on your device as always.
